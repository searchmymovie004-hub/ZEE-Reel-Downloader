Privacy & responsible use

NEXORA does not ask for Instagram passwords or login credentials. Download history is stored only in your browser and can be cleared at any time.

Temporary media

Media processed by the service is stored temporarily to provide MP4 and MP3 downloads, then automatically cleaned up after the configured expiry period.

Public content only

Use NEXORA only for public content you created or have permission to save. Private, login-required, restricted, or copyrighted material without permission is not supported.

Community data

Anonymous ratings and download counters are stored in the configured Firebase project. Do not include personal information in reviews.
