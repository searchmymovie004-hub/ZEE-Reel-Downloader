04 / FAQ

Good to
know.

What links can I download?+

ZEE supports publicly accessible Instagram Reel URLs only. Private accounts, login-required content, and restricted media are not supported.

Is my Instagram account needed?+

No. ZEE never asks for your Instagram password or account credentials.

Why did processing fail?+

Instagram can change its delivery systems, or a Reel may be unavailable, private, or restricted. Please verify the link and try again.

Can I download any content?+

Only download content you created or have permission to save. Respect copyright, privacy, and Instagram’s terms of use.
